The Only True God

Monday, 9 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

1 Kings 18:39 (KJV) – “And when all the people saw it, they fell on their faces: and they said, The LORD, he is the God; the LORD, he is the God.”

God's word validates that there is only one true God. Please understand that on Mount Carmel there were 450 prophets of Baal, but only one God answered by fire. In Babylon, the three Hebrew boys stood before the fire, and the only true God showed up in the fire with them. Daniel 3:28-29 says, "there is no other God that can deliver after this sort." No matter how many idols men trust, only one God delivers. The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from fear to absolute confidence. How to trust the only true God?

1. CALL ON HIM, HE ANSWERS BY FIRE. “Hear me, O LORD, hear me, that this people may know that thou art the LORD God” (1 Kings 18:37). Baal was silent, but the LORD answered. Idols are silent, men fail, systems collapse, but the only true God answers. Your prayer will not go unanswered.

2. STAND FOR HIM, HE WILL STAND WITH YOU. “Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who hath sent his angel, and delivered his servants that trusted in him” (Dan. 3:28). They stood for God in the fire, and God stood with them in the fire. When you stand for the only true God, He will stand with you in your fire.

Beloved, your confidence can be absolute because your God is the only true God.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, the LORD, He is the God! He will answer me by fire in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Idols are silent, but the only true God answers by fire. Put your absolute confidence in Him.”
