Operating in the Supernatural by the Power of the Word

Friday, 6 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Exodus 1:7 (KJV) – “And the children of Israel were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty; and the land was filled with them.”

God's word validates that the Word makes men multiply even under affliction. Please understand that Pharaoh tried to stop them, but the Word made them exceedingly mighty. No matter the oppression, when the Word is at work, the supernatural takes over. The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from natural struggle to supernatural supply. How to operate in the supernatural?

1. SPEAK THE WORD. “And the children of Israel were fruitful” (Exod. 1:7). Fruitfulness is a product of the Word. Don't speak your problem, speak the Word. What you speak is what you multiply.

2. GO AND PREACH, SIGNS WILL FOLLOW. “Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature... And these signs shall follow them that believe” (Mark 16:15,17). Your WhatsApp, your website, your classroom are your pulpit. When you release the Word, signs follow - healing, provision, students, favour follow.

Beloved, the supernatural is not for special people, it is for speaking people.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I multiply exceedingly. Signs and wonders follow me in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “The Word in your mouth is the supernatural in your hand.”
