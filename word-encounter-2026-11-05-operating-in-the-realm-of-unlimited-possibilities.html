Operating in the Realm of Unlimited Possibilities

Thursday, 5 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

John 14:12 (KJV) – “Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do; because I go unto my Father.”

God's word validates that there is no limit to what a believer can do. Please understand that Jesus did not say lesser works, He said greater works. No matter your location in Abua/Odual, your background, or your past failure, your possibility is unlimited in Christ. The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from limitation to manifestation. How to operate in unlimited possibilities?

1. BELIEVE FOR GREATER. “He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works” (John 14:12). Possibility flows in the direction of believers. If you believe for small, you will get small. If you believe for greater, you will see greater.

2. PRESS TOWARD THE MARK. “I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus” (Phil. 3:14). Unlimited is not for those who sit, it is for those who press. Press in prayer, press in work, press in faith. Your pressing determines your possessing.

Beloved, your limit is not your account, your limit is your belief.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, every limit is broken. I enter the realm of unlimited possibilities in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Limitation is a mindset, not a destiny. In Christ, all things are possible.”
