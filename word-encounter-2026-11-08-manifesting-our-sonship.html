Manifesting Our Sonship

Sunday, 8 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Isaiah 8:18 (KJV) – “Behold, I and the children whom the LORD hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the LORD of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.”

God's word validates that sons are for signs and wonders. Please understand that you are not a slave begging for what belongs to your Father, you are a son manifesting what belongs to your Father. No matter your background, once you received Christ, you received power to become a son. John 1:12 says, "But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God." The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from fear to manifestation. How to manifest sonship?

1. KNOW YOUR IDENTITY. “To them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name” (John 1:12). Sonship is not by works, it is by receiving. You did not earn it, you received it. A son does not beg, a son possesses.

2. MANIFEST AS SIGNS AND WONDERS. “I and the children whom the LORD hath given me are for signs and for wonders” (Isa. 8:18). A hidden son is a wasted son. Your sonship must be seen in your confidence, in your giving, in your teaching, in your OPC Academy. The world is waiting for your manifestation.

Beloved, you are not ordinary. You are a son for signs.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I manifest my sonship. I am for signs and wonders in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Sonship is not a title, it is a manifestation. Sons don't hide, they shine.”
