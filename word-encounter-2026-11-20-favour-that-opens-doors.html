Favour That Opens Doors

Friday, 20 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Psalm 5:12 (KJV) – “For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.”

Divine favour achieves effortlessly what human labour struggles to attain. In Genesis 39, Joseph enjoyed magnetic favour inside Potiphar's house and within prison walls. In Esther 2, Esther commanded unprecedented grace before all who saw her, transitioning from obscurity to royalty. In Luke 2:52, Jesus grew steadily in favour with both God and man. No matter how many doors have slammed shut, one stroke of divine favour can completely rewrite your story. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from struggling to standing out. How to walk in favour?

1. CARRY FAVOUR WITH CONFIDENCE. “With favour wilt thou compass him” (Psalm 5:12). Favour surrounds you like a shield. Walk like someone God has chosen. You are not ordinary.

2. USE FAVOUR TO SERVE, NOT TO SHOW OFF. “Let your light so shine” (Matthew 5:16). Favour is not for pride. It is for purpose. When God lifts you, lift others.

Beloved, this week, doors will open for you. The God who favours will distinguish you.

Reflect: What closed door will you ask God to open with favour this week?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I am favoured. Doors are opening. Men and women will help me in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Favour is your shield. Doors are opening for you.”
