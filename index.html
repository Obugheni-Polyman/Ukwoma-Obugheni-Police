Featured Research: PhD Thesis

A Psychological Study of the Impact of Cultural Barriers on Pastoral Counseling Effectiveness in Buguma City, Nigeria

Ukwoma Obugheni Police · EIBCS2526P012 · June 2026
El-Elyon International Bible College, submitted to El-Elyon Seminary

Setting: the 39 Polo of Buguma City, Asari-Toru LGA, Rivers State, a Kalabari cultural setting.

Method: descriptive survey of pastoral counselors and congregants in PFN and CAN churches. Sample of 407 respondents (53.1% female, 46.9% male) using the Taro Yamane formula; questionnaire and interview guide; Multicultural Counseling Theory and African Psychology.

Summary of findings

  • Cultural beliefs, including riverine water-deity worship, were associated with a preference for traditional help over pastoral counseling.
  • Stigma, respect for elders and fear of community disclosure reduced willingness to open up to counselors.
  • Preference for tradition shaped how legitimate counseling was seen when it conflicted with custom.
  • Language mattered: respondents found it easier to express deep feelings in Kalabari than in English.
  • Overall, cultural barriers had a statistically significant effect on perceived counseling effectiveness. The study measured perceptions and help-seeking, not church attendance or clinical outcomes.

Recommendations

Use Kalabari for emotional disclosure, train counselors in multicultural competence, involve respected elders as bridge-builders, provide confidential spaces, and document indigenous coping alongside pastoral care.

Course 1: EV Charging Station Business Beginner · 2 weeks

Locations where vehicles stay parked for a while, such as churches, schools and markets, can suit charging services as electric vehicles become more common. This course shows you how to plan and run one responsibly.

Module A: Site selection

  • Space requirements and access
  • Grid power availability (PHED)
  • Security and CCTV

Module B: Equipment and power

  • AC chargers and solar with inverter and battery backup
  • Signage, payments and CAC registration

Module C: Income models

  • Charging fees
  • Waiting-time services such as water and snacks
  • Secure parking with charging

Module D: Operations and team

  • Training youth attendants
  • Safety, record keeping and customer care

Startup budget templates are provided in class. Figures are estimates that vary by supplier and location, and no income is guaranteed. High-voltage safety training is required.

Course 2: EV Battery and Tyre Service Technician · 2 weeks

Electric vehicles skip engine oil, fuel filters, spark plugs and exhausts, but they need regular battery health checks, cooling system checks and EV-rated tyres. Learn to offer these services safely.

Technical skills

  • Battery health diagnostics and cell balancing basics
  • Cooling system checks
  • EV tyre selection, sales and fitting

Business and safety

  • Setting up a service workshop and sourcing tools from verified suppliers
  • Recurring maintenance plans for fleet operators
  • High-voltage safety: insulated tools, PPE and lockout procedures

Tool lists and service fees discussed are examples only. Verify suppliers yourself. No job or income is guaranteed.

Course 3: Train the Trainers, OPC EV Academy Advanced · 2 weeks + certificate

For technicians, educators and entrepreneurs who want to teach EV skills in their own communities.

Week 1: Theory (online, private group)

  • Day 1: EV and high-voltage safety
