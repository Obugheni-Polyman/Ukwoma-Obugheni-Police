Strength When You Are Tired

Tuesday, 17 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Isaiah 40:31 (KJV) – “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.”

Tiredness is never the final verdict on your capacity. In 1 Kings 19, Elijah was exhausted and wanted to quit, but heaven sustained him with supernatural provision for the journey. In Matthew 11:28, Jesus extended the ultimate invitation: “Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” In 2 Corinthians 12:9, His grace remains sufficient, perfecting His strength in our human weakness. No matter how drained you feel, God is your renewer. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from burnout to boldness. How to find strength when you are tired?

1. WAIT, DON'T QUIT. “They that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength” (Isaiah 40:31). Waiting is not wasting time. It is plugging into God's power. Pause, pray, breathe. Strength is renewed in His presence.

2. RUN ON HIS STRENGTH, NOT YOURS. “Walk, and not faint” (Isaiah 40:31). You were never meant to carry life alone. Lean on Him. His strength carries you when your strength fails.

Beloved, you will not faint this week. The God who renews eagles will renew you.

Reflect: Where do you feel tired today, and what will you surrender to God for renewal?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, my strength is renewed. I will run and not be weary, I will walk and not faint in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “His strength is your strength. You will not faint.”
