Grace For A New Beginning

Monday, 16 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Lamentations 3:22-23 (KJV) – “It is of the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.”

Today is not a continuation of yesterday's failure. His mercies are new. In Genesis 1, God began again with light in darkness. In John 8, the woman caught in sin received a new beginning, not condemnation. In 2 Corinthians 5:17, anyone in Christ is a new creature. No matter what last week brought, God has given you a fresh page. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from regret to renewal. How to start this week with grace?

1. LET GO OF YESTERDAY. “Remember ye not the former things” (Isaiah 43:18). You cannot walk into a new week carrying old guilt. Release the mistake, the delay, the disappointment. Grace is your fresh start.

2. RECEIVE TODAY'S MERCY. “Great is thy faithfulness” (Lamentations 3:23). God has already packaged strength, favour and wisdom for today. Receive it by faith. Mercy is your daily supply.

Beloved, start the week with grace. The God who was faithful last week will be faithful this week.

Reflect: What is one thing from last week you will release today, and trust God for a new beginning?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, this week is new. I receive fresh mercy, fresh strength and fresh favour in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Grace is your fresh start. Today is new.”
