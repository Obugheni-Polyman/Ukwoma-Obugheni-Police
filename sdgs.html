How we contribute

SDG 4: Quality Education

Education is the foundation of our work. We teach practical, usable skills through online courses with certificates, free tools such as the IEP Goal Builder, and guides for parents, teachers and students.

See: Training catalog · Blog · Resources

SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

Safety and well-being go together. Our safety course helps schools, churches and workplaces prevent accidents and plan for emergencies, and our research looks at how culture and stigma affect people seeking counseling.

See: Safety training · Research

SDG 5: Gender Equality

Our courses and programmes are open to women and men, and our community empowerment programmes include women participants. We want more women to gain skills and economic opportunity.

See: Our work in pictures

SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

Our EV training teaches how to plan and run charging services, including solar with battery backup as an option, and our donation page supports a solar project for worship.

See: EV courses · EV charging checklist · Solar project

SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

Skills create livelihoods. We train people for work and small business, support CAC registration in our Nigerian EV packages and build websites for small businesses. We do not guarantee income or employment.

See: Training · Website setup

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Change happens locally. We help schools, churches and workplaces plan for safety and emergencies, and we support clean transport services such as EV charging in the communities where people live and work.

See: Safety training · Services

SDG 13: Climate Action

Our EV and clean energy training supports a shift from petrol and diesel to electric transport, with practical, low-cost steps that individuals and small businesses can take.

See: EV courses · EV Business Estimator

SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

No one achieves the goals alone. We work with schools, churches, researchers, businesses and community leaders. Research produces evidence, education shares it and partnerships help it reach more people.

See: Services · Contact us

Our work in inclusive education also supports SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities). We describe how our work contributes to these goals. We are a small enterprise, and we do not claim to achieve any goal alone.

About the founder

Ukwoma Obugheni Police is the founder of Obugheni-Polyman Consulting Services (CAC BN 9903940), based in Abua/Odual, Rivers State. An educator, researcher and community empowerment advocate, the founder is the author of the PhD thesis on cultural barriers to pastoral counseling in Buguma City featured on our research page.

The aim is simple: to help people learn safely, work with dignity, move cleanly and lead change in their own communities.
