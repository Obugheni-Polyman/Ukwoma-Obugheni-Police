Our Services

Evidence-based consulting, research, special education, training and website services from Obugheni-Polyman Consulting Services (CAC BN 9903940), based in Abua/Odual, Rivers State.

1. Consulting for schools, NGOs and organizations

We help schools, NGOs and private organizations make decisions backed by evidence.

  • Educational needs assessment: understand where your school or programme stands and what to improve first.
  • School needs assessment: a practical look at teaching, support for learners and school systems.
  • Professional development and training: workshops for teachers, school leaders, NGO staff and teams.
  • Programme support: planning, monitoring tools and simple reports your funders and board can understand.

2. Research and academic support

  • Research design and questionnaire design for students, schools and organizations.
  • Data analysis and evidence synthesis: clear tables, findings and plain-language summaries.
  • Academic writing support and proofreading: we guide you so you can write, understand and defend your own work. We do not write projects for you to submit as your own.
  • Field surveys: professional on-site survey services. We quote first, and you can book your site visit with a deposit.
  • Our own research: read about our featured study on cultural barriers to pastoral counseling in Buguma City.

3. Special education and inclusive learning

  • Learning needs assessment covering reading, writing, maths, memory, attention and learning style, with a clear summary for parents and schools.
  • Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) with measurable goals.
  • Inclusive education support for classrooms and whole schools.
  • Educator and parent training with practical strategies for Nigerian classrooms.
  • Ready-made tools: the IEP Toolkit, IEP Goals E-book and Premium Toolkit in our shop, and the free IEP Goal Builder on the home page.

4. OPC Green Mobility Training Academy

Three two-week courses, taught online, with a certificate of completion from Obugheni-Polyman Consulting:

  • Course 1, Beginner: EV Charging Station Business.
  • Course 2, Technician: EV Battery and Tyre Service.
  • Course 3, Advanced: Train the Trainers, OPC EV Academy.

High-voltage safety training is required, and no income or employment is guaranteed. See the course details and fees or go straight to registration.

5. Website and domain setup

Professional websites for schools, churches, NGOs and small businesses, with a WhatsApp chat button and a secure (HTTPS) connection.

  • Basic: one page.
  • Standard: four pages, with a contact form and basic SEO.
  • Premium: six to eight pages with an online store and Paystack payments.

The domain is registered in your name, and the first year is included. We aim to deliver within 48 hours after we receive your payment and all your content. See website packages.

6. Faith and community resources

  • The daily Word Encounter devotional.
  • Free tools on the home page: Daily Word, Prayer Points and the Church Counseling Readiness Check.
  • The Faith and Growth Resources e-book in our shop.

How we work

  1. Tell us what you need. Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form.
  2. We discuss and quote. You get a clear scope and price before you commit.
  3. Payment is confirmed. We confirm your payment before work or access begins.
  4. We deliver and support you. You get the agreed work, with follow-up where it was agreed.
