Inclusive Classroom Management and Practical IEP Implementation (ICIEP-202)

The exact steps to understand learning needs, write realistic IEPs and manage a classroom that includes every learner. Taught online.

Many classrooms were built for one way of learning, and many learners are left behind. This course gives teachers, school owners and parents the practical steps to understand learning needs, write realistic Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) and manage a classroom that works for every learner. It supports Sustainable Development Goal 4 (quality education).

Course at a glance

  • Who it is for: special education teachers, mainstream class teachers, school owners and head teachers, and parents supporting a child with learning or developmental needs.
  • Duration and delivery: one week, taught fully online. Self-paced online modules plus two live interactive question-and-answer webinars. Your class link is sent on WhatsApp after your payment is confirmed.
  • Fee: ₦35,000 for Nigeria, or $27 USD for international participants. This includes the Special Education and IEP Premium Toolkit. Pay by bank transfer, then register.
  • Certificate: certificate of completion from Obugheni-Polyman Consulting.

Module 1: Foundations of inclusive education in Nigeria

  • Understanding learning differences and disabilities, such as ADHD, autism, dyslexia, speech and language delays and physical impairments.
  • Moving from separate provision to classrooms designed for all learners, using Universal Design for Learning (UDL).
  • Facing the stigma around special needs in our communities.

Module 2: Understanding a learner's strengths and challenges

  • Baseline observation: what the child can do now, and in which settings.
  • Working with parents and specialists to build an accurate picture of the child's history.
  • Telling the difference between a learning need and a behaviour problem, and knowing when to refer.

Module 3: Writing effective IEPs

  • The parts of an IEP: learner profile, present levels, goals, accommodations and review.
  • Writing SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) that fit the individual learner.
  • Choosing practical accommodations such as extra time, visual aids and movement or sensory breaks.

Module 4: Managing the inclusive classroom and supporting behaviour

  • Positive behaviour support plans instead of punishment alone.
  • Arranging the classroom to reduce overload and increase engagement.
  • Helping classmates understand and support each other.

What you receive

  • The Special Education and IEP Premium Toolkit: templates, request letters, meeting and tracking sheets, a bank of 50 SMART goals and a glossary.
  • Printable visual schedule and behaviour tracker templates.
  • Certificate of completion.
