The Right Word in a Season

Saturday, 7 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Job 6:25 (KJV) – “How forcible are right words! but what doth your arguing reprove?”

God's word validates that there is power in the right word spoken in the right season. Please understand that one right word can change a life, while many wrong words can destroy it. No matter how long you have been silent, your season has a word. The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from complaining to declaring. How to release the right word?

1. SPEAK WHAT YOU WANT TO SEE. “How forcible are right words!” (Job 6:25). Don't say the negative things you have seen, say the positive things you want to see. Your mouth is your creative tool. What you say is what you see.

2. MANIFEST TRIUMPH EVERYWHERE. “Now thanks be unto God, which always causeth us to triumph in Christ, and maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge by us in every place” (2 Cor. 2:14). Your word is a fragrance. When you speak right, triumph follows you in every place - in Port Harcourt, in Buguma, in your business.

Beloved, somebody is waiting for your word. Don't keep silent in this season.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, my mouth releases the right word. I triumph in every place in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “The right word in the right season is more powerful than a thousand arguments.”
