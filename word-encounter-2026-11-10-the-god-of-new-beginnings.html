The God of New Beginnings

Tuesday, 10 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Isaiah 43:19 (KJV) – “Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.”

God's word validates that He is the God of new beginnings. Please understand that Israel was in captivity, but God said behold. In Genesis 1, the earth was without form and void, and God said let there be light. In John 11, Lazarus was dead for four days, and God said come forth. No matter what happened last week or last month, God is not limited by your past. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from old pain to fresh praise. How to enter a new beginning?

1. EXPECT FRESH DOORS. “Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth” (Isaiah 43:19). Expect fresh doors, fresh ideas, and fresh strength. God does not recycle your yesterday, He releases a new thing. What you expect, you will experience.

2. OPEN YOUR EYES TO WHAT GOD IS DOING. “Shall ye not know it?” (Isaiah 43:19). Many miss the new thing because they are still looking at the old thing. Open your eyes. The way in the wilderness is already being made.

Beloved, this week is not a repeat of last week. God is doing a new thing.

Reflect: What old thing are you still looking at that God has already moved past?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, God is doing a new thing in my life. I will see it and walk in it in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “God is not limited by your past. Expect the new thing He is doing.”
