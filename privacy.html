Privacy Notice

Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Last updated 10 November 2026.

We collect your name, email and phone number only to process orders and enrollments, send download links and course access, and reply to messages. We do not sell your data. Advertising cookies are set by Google, not by us. Ad and analytics cookies stay off until you accept them in the cookie notice, and you can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

  • Paystack and Selar: process payments and collect your email and payment details. We never see card details.
  • Formspree and Google Forms: deliver your contact messages and course registrations to us. Our backup registration form uses Google Forms, which keeps your answers and any uploaded receipt in our Google account. A registration includes your name, WhatsApp number, email, country, payment reference, uploaded payment receipt and proposed business name.
  • Google Analytics: counts visits using anonymized data such as pages viewed and device type.
  • Google Translate and UserWay: provide page translation and accessibility tools on our home page and may collect usage data.
  • Google AdSense: this site shows ads served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can manage or opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you click a WhatsApp link, your messages go through WhatsApp. Payment receipts you send us there are used only to confirm your payment.

To ask about, correct or delete your information, email InfoOPCww@obugheni-polyman.com.ng. You can limit analytics through your browser settings.
