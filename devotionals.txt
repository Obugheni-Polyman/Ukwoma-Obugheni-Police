Acts 14:11; John 3:8 – “The gods are come down to us in the likeness of men.”
To be flesh is to be seen, but to be spirit is to be real. Your flesh is not you, it is your address. Your spirit is the real you, only wearing flesh to operate on earth. God did not make you a body with a spirit, He made you a spirit with a body!
When the people of Lystra saw Paul heal a man crippled from birth, they shouted "The gods are come down to us in the likeness of men!" They misread what they saw: they took Paul and Barnabas for gods visiting in human form (Acts 14:12). Paul corrected them, "We also are men of like passions" (Acts 14:15). Yet the power at work through him was real. He was a man filled with the Spirit, a spirit man living in a body!
Many believers today live inside-out. They live from flesh trying to reach spirit. They feed the body, pamper the flesh, but starve the spirit. They are conscious of body, but unconscious of spirit. But you were not redeemed to live as flesh managing spirit, you were redeemed to live as spirit governing flesh! John 3:8 – "So is every one that is born of the Spirit."
What does it mean to be a spirit being in the likeness of flesh?
1. YOU MUST KNOW THAT FLESH IS YOUR TABERNACLE, NOT YOUR IDENTITY. Your body is your earthly tabernacle (2 Cor 5:1), your suit to function on earth. Just like you wear a shirt, your spirit wears your body. Honour it, for it is the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Cor 6:19), but do not mistake it for who you are. Your height, your complexion and your tribe are your likeness, not your limit! Jesus came in the likeness of men (Phil. 2:7) but was not limited by men. You may be in the likeness of your family background, but you are not limited by their history. Likeness is your entry point for manifestation, not your prison for limitation!
2. YOU MUST LIVE LIKE WIND, NOT LIKE WOOD. "The wind bloweth where it listeth... so is every one that is born of the Spirit" (John 3:8). Wood is visible, heavy and stuck in one place. Wind is invisible, powerful and moves where it wills! A spirit being cannot be caged by economy, cannot be stopped by your village or background, cannot be explained by logic. People may not see your spirit, but they must hear the sound! The sound of your wisdom, the sound of your results, the sound of your faith! You may be in the flesh, but you are not of the flesh. You are wind in flesh suit!
Beloved, stop trying to be God in flesh, start manifesting as son in flesh! You are not a god that visited, you are a son that was raised! Creation is not waiting for gods to come down, creation is waiting for sons to manifest (Rom 8:19). You are that spirit being God has positioned in Port Harcourt, in Buguma, in your office to shift atmospheres!
Prophetic Declaration: I decree today, I am a spirit being in the likeness of flesh. My flesh is my tabernacle, not my identity. I live from spirit to flesh. I move like wind, I cannot be caged, I cannot be stopped. I manifest as son on earth in Jesus name. Amen and Amen.
EMPOWERMENT 🗝️: “Flesh is what men see, but spirit is who you are - let your spirit lead your flesh, not your flesh lead your spirit.”

OVERCOMER'S MENTALITY
1 John 5:4 – “For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.”
To be a survivor is to escape with wounds, but to be an overcomer is to conquer with victory. God did not redeem you to manage defeat, He redeemed you to dominate it. In Christ, you are not trying to overcome, you HAVE overcome!
Many believers today live with a survival mentality. They survive Monday, they survive sickness, they survive betrayal, they survive hardship. They are just coping, managing, enduring. But God did not call you to be a survivor, He called you to be an overcomer! A survivor says "I made it through," but an overcomer says "I conquered it through Christ!"
What does it mean to have an overcomer's mentality?
1. HE REFUSES VICTIM MENTALITY AND CHOOSES VICTOR MENTALITY. Victim mentality always asks "Why me? Why is this happening to me? Who is responsible for my pain?" Victim mentality blames, complains and isolates. But overcomer's mentality asks "What is God teaching me here? How is God making me stronger through this?" (Romans 8:28). Joseph had overcomer's mentality in prison - he did not say "my brothers betrayed me," he said "God meant it for good." David had overcomer's mentality before Goliath - he did not say "he is too big," he said "who is this uncircumcised Philistine?" When you walk in light, you stop seeing yourself as a victim of people and start seeing yourself as a victor in Christ.
2. HE LIVES BY FAITH AND NOT BY FEELINGS. "This is the victory that overcometh the world, even our FAITH" (1 Jn. 5:4). Your feelings will tell you that you are defeated, that you are finished, that you cannot make it. But your faith tells you that you are more than a conqueror! Feelings change, faith remains. Feelings are based on what you see, faith is based on what God says. An overcomer does not deny the battle, he denies defeat in the battle. You may be in the battle, but the battle is not in you! You walk by faith that the blood is still cleansing you, God is still with you, and your path is shining brighter and brighter.
Beloved, stop managing your problem and start overcoming it! Stop surviving the day and start conquering the day! Your background did not stop you, your mistakes did not stop you, your enemies cannot stop you. You have an overcomer's mentality!
Prophetic Declaration: I decree today, I refuse survival mentality, I receive overcomer's mentality. I am not a victim, I am a victor. I do not survive, I overcome. My faith overcomes sickness, sin, failure and every attack in Jesus name. Amen and Amen.
EMPOWERMENT 🗝️: “Survivors count what they lost in the battle, overcomers count what God built in them through the battle.”
Be Empowered!
Ukwoma Obugheni P.
