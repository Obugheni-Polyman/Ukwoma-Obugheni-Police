Wisdom For Every Decision

Wednesday, 18 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

James 1:5 (KJV) – “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.”

You do not have to guess your way through life's intricate mazes. In 1 Kings 3, Solomon asked for wisdom and God added wealth and honour to his portfolio. In Daniel 2, divine secrets were unveiled to Daniel in the place of prayer. In Proverbs 3:5-6, the divine guarantee is clear: trust Him wholeheartedly, and He will direct your paths. No matter how confusing a decision looks, heaven holds the blueprint. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from confusion to clarity. How to decide with wisdom?

1. ASK BEFORE YOU ACT. “Let him ask of God” (James 1:5). Don't decide in panic. Don't decide in anger. Ask God first. Prayer is your first strategy, not your last resort.

2. FOLLOW PEACE, NOT PRESSURE. “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet” (Psalm 119:105). God's wisdom brings peace, even when the step is bold. If there is no peace, wait. If there is peace, move.

Beloved, you will not miss your way. The God who guides will order your steps this week.

Reflect: What decision have you been struggling with, and will you commit it to God today?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I have wisdom. I will not make a costly mistake. My steps are ordered in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Wisdom is your advantage. Ask, and you will receive.”
