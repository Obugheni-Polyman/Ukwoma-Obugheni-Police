Occupational Safety and Emergency Preparedness (OSEP-101)

Practical steps to prevent hazards, plan for a crisis and protect lives in your school, church or workplace. Taught online.

Accidents, fires and medical emergencies can happen in any school, church or workplace. This course gives your staff practical steps to prevent hazards, plan for a crisis and respond calmly until trained help arrives. It is taught fully online, so your team can join from anywhere.

Course at a glance

  • Who it is for: school owners and administrators, teachers, HR and facility managers, safety officers and church leadership teams in Rivers State and across Nigeria.
  • Duration and delivery: two days, taught fully online. Day 1 covers the theory. Day 2 is practical exercises, case studies and planning. Your class link is sent on WhatsApp after your payment is confirmed.
  • Fee: ₦20,000 for Nigeria, or $15 USD for international participants. Pay by bank transfer, then register.
  • Certificate: certificate of completion from Obugheni-Polyman Consulting. It is not a government licence or a statutory certification.

Module 1: Fundamentals of workplace and school safety

  • Finding out which safety rules and standards apply to your type of school or workplace, and which authorities to check with.
  • Spotting physical, ergonomic, chemical and psychological hazards in classrooms, offices and worship centres.
  • Building a safety culture with daily checklists and simple risk assessments.

Module 2: Fire safety and evacuation

  • The fire triangle (oxygen, heat and fuel) and common causes of fire.
  • Types of fire extinguisher, which fire each one is for, and how to use one (the PASS method: Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep), explained with diagrams and demonstrations.
  • How to check the extinguishers at your own site: where they are, whether they are in date and whether they are easy to reach.
  • Planning an evacuation: exit routes, assembly points and roles, and how to run a drill at your own premises using our checklist.

Module 3: Emergencies and first aid awareness

  • Recognising an emergency, and how to call for help quickly and clearly.
  • Awareness of common emergencies, such as bleeding, burns, fainting, suspected fractures, choking and cardiac arrest, and why early help matters.
  • Setting up a stocked first aid box and an emergency contact list.
  • Knowing your limits: when to act, and when to wait for trained help.

Module 4: Crisis communication and review after an incident

  • Staying calm and directing people in an orderly way.
  • Working with emergency responders, security staff and community leaders.
  • Reviewing what happened afterwards so it does not happen again.

Day 2 practical work

Using real situations from schools, churches and offices, each participant prepares a site risk checklist, a simple evacuation plan and an emergency contact list, and gets feedback in the live session.

What you receive

  • Participant workbook and emergency checklist template.
  • First aid logbook template.
  • Certificate of completion.

Please note

Online training cannot replace hands-on practice. This course builds awareness, planning and confidence. It is not first aid, CPR or fire warden certification. If your organization or regulator requires hands-on certification, we recommend a local accredited provider, such as the Nigerian Red Cross Society. In a real emergency, always call your local emergency services.
