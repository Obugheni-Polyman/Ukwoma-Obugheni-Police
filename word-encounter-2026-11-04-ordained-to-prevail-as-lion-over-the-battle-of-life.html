Ordained to Prevail as Lion over the Battle of Life

Wednesday, 4 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Rev. 5:5 (KJV) – “And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.”

God's word validates that you are not ordained to lose, you are ordained to prevail. Please understand that the Lion in you has already prevailed before the battle started. No matter the battle of life - family, finances, OPC Academy, ministry - you were sent into it as Jesus was sent. John 17:18 says, "As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I sent them into the world." The understanding of this truth will help anyone reposition from fear to roaring. How to prevail as a lion?

1. STOP WEEPING, START ROARING. “Weep not: behold, the Lion... hath prevailed” (Rev. 5:5). Weeping is for those who have no victory. You have victory. A lion does not cry for identity, he roars and the jungle knows. Whatever has made you cry has already been defeated.

2. KNOW YOU ARE SENT. “As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I sent them” (John 17:18). You did not send yourself, God sent you. A sent man cannot be stopped by any battle. Your sending is your security.

Beloved, the battle is not to break you, it is to reveal the Lion in you.

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I prevail over every battle of life. I roar and my enemies scatter in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “A lion does not struggle for victory, he manifests victory. You are ordained to prevail.”
