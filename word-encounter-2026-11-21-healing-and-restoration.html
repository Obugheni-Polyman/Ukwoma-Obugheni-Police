Healing And Restoration

Saturday, 21 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Jeremiah 30:17 (KJV) – “For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the LORD.”

God is an expert at mending what life has broken. In Job 42, divine restoration compensated Job with double for all his trouble. In Joel 2:25, a sweeping promise guarantees the recovery of years devoured by the locusts. In Luke 8, a single touch of faith made the woman with the issue of blood completely whole. No matter what has suffered loss—whether health, peace, relationship, or finances—the Creator can restore it. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from brokenness to wholeness. How to receive restoration?

1. BRING YOUR WOUND TO GOD. “I will heal thee of thy wounds” (Jeremiah 30:17). Don't hide the pain. Don't pretend you are fine. Healing begins when you are honest with God.

2. EXPECT RESTORATION, NOT JUST SURVIVAL. “I will restore health unto thee” (Jeremiah 30:17). God does not just patch you up. He restores you. Expect better than before.

Beloved, your story is not over. The God who restores will make you whole again.

Reflect: What area of your life needs restoration, and will you bring it to God today?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, I am healed and restored. What was lost is returning double in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “Restoration is your portion. You will be whole again.”
