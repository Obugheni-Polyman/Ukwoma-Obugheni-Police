About Obugheni-Polyman Consulting

Practical, evidence-based solutions for schools, churches, NGOs and small businesses in Rivers State, Nigeria, and beyond.

Who we are

Obugheni-Polyman Consulting Services is a micro enterprise registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), BN 9903940, based in Abua/Odual, Rivers State. We serve Buguma City, Port Harcourt and beyond, and much of our work is delivered online.

Our mission

To help communities thrive spiritually, professionally and environmentally through evidence-based counseling, safety training and clean transport solutions.

What we do

  • Consulting: educational needs assessment, and professional development and training for schools, NGOs and private organizations.
  • Research: research design, data analysis, academic writing and evidence synthesis. Our featured study examines cultural barriers to pastoral counseling in Buguma City.
  • Special education: inclusive education and learning support, assessment, Individualized Education Plans, and educator and parent training.
  • Training: the OPC Training Academy offers five online courses: EV charging, battery and tyre service, training the trainers, occupational safety and emergency preparedness, and inclusive classroom management with IEPs. See the course catalog.
  • Faith resources: the daily Word Encounter devotional and free tools for churches and classrooms.

Founder

The work is led by Ukwoma Obugheni Police, author of the Word Encounter devotionals and of the PhD thesis A Psychological Study of the Impact of Cultural Barriers on Pastoral Counseling Effectiveness in Buguma City, Nigeria.
