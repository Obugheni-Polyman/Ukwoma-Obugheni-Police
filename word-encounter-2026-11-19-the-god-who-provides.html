The God Who Provides

Thursday, 19 November 2026 · By Ukwoma Obugheni P.

Philippians 4:19 (KJV) – “But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.”

Your financial security and provision are independent of economic fluctuations. In Genesis 22, Jehovah-Jireh provided a ram for Abraham on Mount Moriah. In Exodus 16, supernatural manna dropped daily in the barren wilderness. In 1 Kings 17, the widow's cruse of oil defied mathematical logic. No matter what current bills or deficits indicate, God remains your ultimate source. The understanding of this truth will help anyone move from worry to worship. How to trust God for provision?

1. LOOK TO THE SOURCE, NOT THE CHANNEL. “My God shall supply” (Philippians 4:19). Your job, business or helper is a channel. God is the source. Channels can dry up, but the Source never runs dry.

2. GIVE THANKS BEFORE YOU RECEIVE. “In every thing give thanks” (1 Thessalonians 5:18). Thanksgiving opens the door for supply. Thank Him for what you have, and trust Him for what is coming.

Beloved, your need is already known. The God who provided yesterday will provide again today.

Reflect: What need will you thank God for in advance today, trusting that He has already made provision?

Prophetic declaration: I decree, my needs are supplied. I will not lack, I will not beg, I will have more than enough in Jesus name. Amen and Amen

Empowerment: “God is your Source. Provision is coming.”
