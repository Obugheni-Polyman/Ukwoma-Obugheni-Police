Terms and Refunds

Last updated 8 November 2026.

  • Payments. Payments are made on Selar or Paystack, where the price is shown before you pay. The price you see when you pay is the price you pay.
  • Digital resources. Files are delivered by email or download link after payment. Because digital files can’t be returned, we don’t refund files that were delivered. If a file is missing, defective or never arrives, contact us within 48 hours with your payment reference and we will fix it or refund you.
  • Thesis PDF. The full thesis is sold as a digital file and follows the digital resources rule above. The free preview shows the title page, abstract and contents so you can check the scope before you buy. The thesis is for your personal and academic use. You may cite it, but please do not copy, resell or share the file publicly without written permission.
  • Monthly access. Each month is paid separately and does not renew automatically. Access ends when the paid month ends.
  • Training courses. We confirm your start date after enrollment. You can ask for a refund, or a move to the next cohort, before the course starts. Once it has started, refunds are at our discretion. If we cancel a course, you receive a full refund or a place in the next cohort. Certificates confirm completion and are not a government or regulatory licence. We do not guarantee income or employment.
  • CAC registration fee (Nigeria). Nigerian course packages include N30,000 for CAC business registration support. If you withdraw before we start your CAC filing, we refund this part. Once filing has started, it is not refundable.
  • Field surveys. The deposit confirms your booking and is credited against your final invoice. Rescheduling and cancellation terms are stated in your quote.
  • Website and domain packages. Work starts after payment and receipt of the content we need, and the delivery time runs from then. The domain is registered in your name; the first year is included and renewals from the second year are paid by you directly to the registrar. Once your site has gone live, payments are not refundable. If we cannot deliver what the package describes, we will fix it or refund you.
  • Donations. Donations are voluntary and not refundable, except where made in error. Contact us promptly if that happens.
  • Refund requests. Email InfoOPCww@obugheni-polyman.com.ng or message us on WhatsApp with your name, the item and your payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • Use of materials. Resources are for your own or your organization’s use. Please don’t resell or share them publicly without our written permission.
  • Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
